## 2. Decision Notes: Real Captures vs. AI Generation

1. **Why Real Captures for ML Work:**  
   AI-generated UI mockups look glossy but lack actual data numbers. Using real cropped screenshots of Jupyter notebook outputs proves genuine execution and builds technical credibility.
2. **Why Real Photo for Profile:**  
   AI avatar generators produce generic synthetic faces. A real personal photo establishes authentic trust with hiring managers.
3. **Where AI Generation Was Used:**  
   AI generation was restricted strictly to abstract background textures matching the Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) and Tech Teal (`#0EA5E9`) identity kit.
