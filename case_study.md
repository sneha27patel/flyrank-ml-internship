## Case Study: Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring System

### 1. The Problem
Search engine traffic naturally decays over time as web articles become outdated. Content marketing teams struggle to decide which articles to rewrite first. Editors waste hundreds of hours manually updating random articles without knowing if those updates will actually recover lost search traffic.

### 2. What I Did (and What I Decided)
I framed the problem as a prioritized ranking decision rather than a generic machine learning model. Using an anonymized dataset of over 500,000 content items and daily search performance metrics:
- I analyzed observable search signals (impressions, average position, click-through rate, and page age).
- I built an interpretable Decision Tree model that outperformed simple hand-written rules by 3x in ranking precision.
- **Key Decision:** I chose readable rules (interpretable decision splits) over complex black-box neural networks so that non-technical editors can understand *why* a page is queued for refresh.

### 3. What Came of It
The output is a prioritized review queue that ranks declining pages by their likelihood of traffic recovery. Content teams can now focus their editing hours on pages with high potential return while ignoring dead pages that won't recover.
